Mystic Radar Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This policy explains what data the Mystic Radar app (developed by Irraxional Team, "we") collects, what it's used for, and what choices you have.

What data we collect

Location (GPS). Mystic Radar needs your location for its core functionality: showing the distance and direction to collectible spheres on the radar. This location:

  • Is used only on your own device, in real time, to calculate the radar.
  • Is never sent to our servers — in fact, we don't have any servers of our own: the app has no backend or cloud database.
  • Is not stored linked to your identity or shared with anyone, beyond what's described in the advertising section below.

Game data. Your progress (spheres found, wishes, achievements, settings, Manual Game sessions) is saved only locally on your device. We don't create user accounts or directly collect your name, email, or any other identifying information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app includes an optional rewarded ad (watch an ad in exchange for in-game help), served by Google AdMob. When an ad is shown, Google may collect advertising identifiers and other technical data under its own privacy policy, for ad measurement and personalization purposes.

Before showing any ad, if you're located in the European Union, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, the app will ask for your consent through Google's standard form (User Messaging Platform). You can change your decision at any time by reopening that form from the app's settings.

More information on how Google handles this data: Google's Privacy Policy and Google's User Consent Policy.

Permissions the app requests

  • Location (approximate and precise): essential for the radar, the game's core feature.
  • Internet: to load rewarded ads and to check map accessibility data (OpenStreetMap) when "Only accessible zones" is enabled.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback when collecting a sphere or earning an achievement.

Data retention and deletion

All progress data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app, or clear its data from Android settings, that information is deleted completely — no copy remains on any server of ours, because we never had one.

Children's privacy

Mystic Radar is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children beyond what's described in this policy.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll publish the new version at this same address with a revised update date.
